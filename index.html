ABOUT PHOENIX

A Platform for Learning, Growth & Opportunity

PHOENIX is a platform designed to help young learners discover their interests, develop practical skills, earn recognised certifications and explore meaningful opportunities for their future.

We believe that every young person has the potential to learn, grow and build a meaningful future when provided with the right opportunities, guidance and support. PHOENIX brings these elements together through a structured journey that connects learning with skills, skills with opportunities, and opportunities with sustainable growth.

Institutional Framework

NALSA: Child-friendly legal services, legal aid, counselling, representation and coordination through State/District Legal Services Institutions.

NCPCR: Protection and promotion of child rights, monitoring of child-rights safeguards and matters concerning children in conflict with law.

This prototype is a proposed coordination model; it does not represent an existing government portal or imply that NALSA and NCPCR jointly administer this software.
